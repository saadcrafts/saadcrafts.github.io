Inside my ICT labs

VirtualizationKali Linux in VMware

I used a virtual machine to run Linux alongside Windows. The question that stuck with me was what happens to the memory when the guest shuts down. The model above lets you test that distinction. The guest’s virtual disk still occupies storage after shutdown.

SystemsBootable Windows media

I used Rufus to prepare a USB installer. The lab connected three steps that can seem unrelated at first: selecting an operating-system image, writing it to the intended USB drive, and choosing that drive in the boot menu.

CommunicationProtecting your family online

I designed an ICT awareness poster for families. I focused on decisions a reader can act on, such as pausing before opening an unexpected link and checking who sent a message through a separate channel.
